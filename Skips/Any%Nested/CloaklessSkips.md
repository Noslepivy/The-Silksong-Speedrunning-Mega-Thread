## Skips and Time Saves used in Key/Flea (Cloakless) routes
The listed skips here are Key-only until Chalamet skip, as Flea and Key get to Widow in different ways, but are near identical after (with the occasional exception of Flea having access to flew brew, such as in Clawline room skip). Movement to get each flea for Flea route is not listed here; for how Flea route gets from Greymoor to Widow, and how to fight Lace 1, refer to what Bells/Mist does in Cloakful skips.

### Act 1
- Boss slowdown menuing
- Chest room
- Bone Bottom elevator momentum stop
- Wormways pogo's
- Chalamet skip
- Mist
- Exhaust Organ steam cycle DDG
- Phantom strats
### Act 2
- Cogwork Dancers climb
- Underworks RP walljump
